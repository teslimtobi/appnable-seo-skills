# Who Appnable is (read before every blog)

Source: appnable.com (homepage, /solutions/, /case-studies/), read October 2026. Files in `company-docs/` override this summary where they differ. Re-check the website if a detail matters.

## In one paragraph

Appnable Ltd is a UK enterprise consultancy, working since 2003, "where documents, compliance and Microsoft 365 meet", for law firms, financial institutions, pharmaceutical companies and government bodies "that cannot afford to get it wrong". Most of its work is in sectors where it "has to stand up to an audit afterwards". Offices: London (Bishopsgate) and Dubai. Track record on the site: 150+ implementations, 20+ years, 100+ global clients, 15+ countries, 95% client retention.

## The five solutions (all on https://www.appnable.com/solutions/)

1. **Custom software:** bespoke platforms, portals and integrations "when the off-the-shelf answer does not fit"; multi-tenant products with real isolation; ongoing monitoring and support. Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Node, PostgreSQL, Azure.
2. **Automation:** "the repetitive work between people and systems, removed, with the judgement left exactly where it belongs". Document generation, review and extraction at volume with measured accuracy; workflow automation across systems; applied AI with evaluation harnesses and cost ceilings; Power Automate and Dataverse where they fit. Stack: Power Automate, Dataverse, Azure Functions, Python.
3. **Microsoft 365 and Copilot:** "two decades inside Office". Copilot readiness (permission and oversharing review before switch-on); Copilot extensibility (declarative agents, plugins, Graph connectors); Word/PowerPoint/Outlook add-ins on Office.js; modernising legacy VBA and VSTO estates; tenant architecture, Entra ID, conditional access, Purview governance. Stack: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Office.js, Microsoft Graph, SharePoint, Entra ID.
4. **ERP and business systems:** moving organisations "off a stack of half-working tools and onto one system". **Odoo** implementation against the real process; consolidating quoting, point of sale, specification, stock and accounting; data migration and reconciliation; phased cutover so the business keeps trading. Appnable implements **Odoo**, not Dynamics 365: never say Appnable implements Dynamics (see note in the guidelines file).
5. **Consulting:** technology roadmaps "with the trade-offs named", process review before tooling decisions, compliance designed in, change management; extra delivery capacity through Wingato.
